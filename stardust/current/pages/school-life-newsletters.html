Newsletters

Mercurius is the school newsletter and is published fortnightly. Mercurius contains news and information about happenings in the Fort Street Community as well as important details about school activities and upcoming events.

Contributions and feedback are most welcome.

Please contact us at: fortstreet-h.school@det.nsw.edu.au (opens in new window)
