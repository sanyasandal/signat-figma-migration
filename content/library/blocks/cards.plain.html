Our vision for student achievement

Fort Street is proud to continue its co-educational traditions. We value the mix of young men and women learning together. We expect students to develop as model citizens and work towards developing individual and collaborative skills and attributes in each student.

Extensive range of student opportunities and activities

At Fort Street High School, we offer a variety of programs in the classroom and across the school to help students build new skills, make friends and explore new interests.

Wellbeing at Fort Street is for all students

A focus on wellbeing goes beyond the welfare needs of a few individual students and aims for all students to be healthy, happy, successful and productive individuals who are active and positive contributors to the school and society in which they live.
