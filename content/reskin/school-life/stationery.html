Stationery

In Year 7 parents are provided with a list of stationery requirements for their child(ren). Parents may choose to purchase these items from any supplier or store of their choosing. Parents may also use the link provided in the form to order from Winc if that is more convenient.

Download the Year 7 stationery list here.

To order through Winc visit wincschools.com.au and follow these four steps.

  • Search for your school, Fort Street High School, Petersham, NSW and enter your school access key 8KB4T if required.
  • Complete the student’s details and select Year 7. Simply select all relevant subjects.
  • Review your requirements list and make changes if required. Please note: some list items have been denoted by your school as a prerequisite for the subject or year.
  • Proceed to checkout. Please note: For home deliveries only, if you have more than one student at the same school, please repeat the above steps.

Please see the stationary list for further details.

From Year 8 onwards parents should work with their child to determine what items need replenishing. Some electives may at times require additional items and these will be requested directly from the teacher if necessary.
